What it does

A calm place for the quotes you care about

Swipe to discover

One quote at a time. Swipe to the next one, or tap Next. Like the ones you love and they are saved for later.

Quote packs

Browse packs by category and choose the ones you want to see. Preview a pack's quotes before you pick it.

Share as an image

Turn any quote into an image in the colors you choose, then share it or save it to your photos.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets bring a quote to you, in a color theme you pick in Settings.

Quote notifications

Turn on notifications to get a quote on a schedule that suits you. You decide whether to turn them on.

Favorites

Every quote you liked, in one list. Swipe to remove one when you are done with it.

Follows your iPhone's light or dark appearance, or choose your own in Settings.

Private by design

Remember keeps things simple and keeps your data yours.

  • No accounts. Remember never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • No ads, and nothing used to track you across other apps or websites.
  • Usage and crash data is off until you say yes, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.
  • The text of the quotes you read, like or share is never collected.
  • Your favorites and settings stay on your device.
