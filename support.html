Frequently asked questions

How do I choose a quote pack?

Open the Browse tab. Use the category chips at the top to filter the list. Tap a pack to preview two of its quotes, then tap “set as package”. You can also use the select button on a pack’s row to choose it straight from the list.

How do I share a quote as an image?

On the Home tab, tap the share button. Pick the colors you like, then share the image or save it to your photos. If saving doesn’t work, check that Remember is allowed to add photos in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security > Photos.

How do I add a widget?

For the Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area, tap the + button, search for Remember and choose a size. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen, tap the area under the time and pick Remember. Lock Screen widgets need iOS 16 or later.

How do I change my widget’s color theme?

Open Settings in Remember and choose a widget theme. If a widget doesn’t update right away, use the widget refresh option in the same section.

How do I turn quote notifications on or off?

Open Settings in Remember and go to Notifications. If notifications don’t arrive, also check the iPhone Settings app under Notifications > Remember.

How do I remove a favorite?

Open the Favorites tab and swipe the quote to delete it.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

In Remember, open Settings > Appearance and choose System, Light or Dark.

How do I turn usage and crash data on or off?

Open Settings > Privacy and use the “Share usage and crash data” toggle. It is off until you agree, and the app works the same either way. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Your favorites and settings are stored on your device, so deleting the app removes them. To stop usage and crash data, turn off the toggle above. To have emails you sent me deleted, write to the address above.

Can I request a quote pack or send feedback?

Yes. In Settings, use “Request a quote pack” or “Send feedback”, which open an email to me. You can also email me directly.

I found a wrong or misattributed quote.

Email me the quote and the name of the pack it is in, and I’ll check and fix it.
