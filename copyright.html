Copyright & Takedown Requests

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Remember displays short quotes, each attributed to its speaker and source. All quotes belong to their respective authors and rights holders. Remember is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

Sending a request

If you believe a quote in the app infringes your copyright, email jeibee.dev@gmail.com with:

  1. your name and contact details;
  2. the work you own;
  3. the quote(s) and the pack name;
  4. a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorized;
  5. a statement that your information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act for them.

What happens next

I review requests promptly and remove content when appropriate.
