Privacy Policy for Remember

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy applies to the Remember app for iPhone (“the Application”), made by Jishnu Biju (“Jeibee”, “I”, “me”). You can contact me at jeibee.dev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Remember has no accounts and doesn’t ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • If you agree, the Application collects anonymous usage data and crash reports to help improve it. This is off until you say yes, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Privacy. If you leave it off and the Application crashes, it may ask whether to send that one crash report, and sends it only if you say yes.
  • The Application never collects the text of quotes you read, like, or share.
  • There are no ads, and nothing is used to track you across other apps or websites.

Information collected, only if you agree

If you choose “Allow” on the first-launch screen, or turn on “Share usage and crash data” in Settings, the Application uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Firebase Analytics collects:

  • Which screens you open and which features you use, for example liking or sharing a quote, choosing a share image style, choosing a quote pack, or changing a setting.
  • Identifiers of the quotes and quote packs involved, never their text.
  • Your settings choices, such as appearance mode, widget theme, widget refresh interval, notification schedule, whether notifications are on, and how many quote packs you have selected.
  • Whether you have a Remember widget on your home screen.
  • Whether you allowed or declined notification permission when the Application asked.
  • Technical information collected automatically by Firebase, such as app version, device model, operating system version, language, and a random app-specific identifier. Firebase also derives an approximate location (country or region) from your IP address. Full IP addresses are not stored in the analytics data.

I use this only to understand which parts of the Application are used so I can improve it. I can’t tell who you are from it.

Firebase Crashlytics collects a crash report when the Application crashes, such as the technical state of the app at the time of the crash, the app version, device model, operating system version, and a random installation identifier. I use these only to find and fix bugs. If you agreed to share usage data, a crash report also includes recent app events of the kind described above. If the setting is off when a crash happens, the report stays on your device and the Application may ask whether you want to send it to me. It is sent only if you choose “Send”. It is deleted if you choose “Don’t send”, and also if you later change the setting.

If you choose “No thanks”, or turn the setting off, the Application stops sending this data, apart from a crash report you choose to send as described above.

Consent and legal basis

For people in the UK, EU, and EEA, I process analytics and crash data only with your consent, which you give by choosing “Allow” or by turning the setting on. Choosing “Send” when the Application offers to send a crash report is your consent to send that one report. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings > Privacy. Withdrawing doesn’t affect processing carried out before you withdrew.

Data stored on your device

Your favorites, selected quote packs, and settings are stored on your device and shared with the Remember widget on the same device. I don’t receive this stored data. If you agreed to share usage data, I receive anonymous events about actions such as liking a quote or choosing a pack, as described above.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, they are scheduled on your device. The text of notifications is not sent to me. If you agreed to share usage data, your notification schedule setting (for example daily or weekdays, and the hour) is included, as described above.

Email

If you email me feedback or a quote pack request, I receive your email address and what you write. I use it only to reply and to improve the Application, and I delete it once it is no longer needed, and within 12 months.

Quotes and copyright

Quotes shown in the Application belong to their respective authors and rights holders. If you are a rights holder and want content removed, see the Copyright & Takedown page.

Third-party services

Analytics and crash reporting are provided by Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics). Google processes the data on my behalf. Its practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

I may disclose information if required by law, such as to comply with a legal process or a government request, or when needed to protect rights, safety, or to investigate fraud.

International data transfers

Google may process analytics and crash data in countries outside your own, including outside the UK and EEA, such as the United States. Where the law requires safeguards for these transfers, Google applies them as described in its terms.

How long data is kept

  • Analytics data: event-level data is kept for 2 months and then deleted automatically. Aggregated statistics that do not relate to any individual, such as total daily users, may be kept longer.
  • Crash reports: kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Emails you send me: deleted when no longer needed, and within 12 months.
  • Data stored on your device: stays until you delete the Application.

Your rights and how to delete your data

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. If you are in the UK or EU, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Because analytics and crash data are anonymous, I usually can’t link it to you. You can stop it at any time:

  • Turn off “Share usage and crash data” in Settings > Privacy to stop further collection. This also clears analytics data stored on your device and resets the random app-specific identifier used for analytics.
  • Delete the Application to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Email me at jeibee.dev@gmail.com to ask for your emails to me to be deleted, or to make any other request. I will respond within the time required by law.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

I do not sell or share your personal information. California residents have the right to know what is collected, to delete it, and to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. To exercise them, email jeibee.dev@gmail.com.

Children

The Application is not intended for children under 16, and I don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and think your child has sent me personal information, contact me at jeibee.dev@gmail.com and I will delete it.

Security

I take reasonable steps to protect information that I handle. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If the Application changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding ads, purchases, or new kinds of data, I will update this page and the effective date, and ask for your consent again where the law requires it. Earlier versions are available on request.
